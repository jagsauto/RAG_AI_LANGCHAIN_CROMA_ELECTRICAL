Electrical Items – Product Descriptions

Bulbs: Our LED range includes E27 and B22 bases in 9W–12W. Suitable for domestic and commercial use. Energy-efficient, long life, available in warm white and daylight.

Cables: Twin & earth and flex in 1mm²–4mm². All cables are PVC insulated, flame retardant, and comply with BS standards. Sold in drums.

Wires: Single-core and multi-core options. Copper conductor. Sizes from 1mm² to 6mm² for lighting, power, and fixed wiring.

Switches: 1-gang and 2-gang, 1-way and 2-way. Plastic and metal finishes. Dimmer switches compatible with LED and halogen. Bell pushes for doorbells.

Sockets: Single and double. USB charging options. Switched and unswitched. 13A, 250V.
